Ant Controls

New ants appear at one of their colony's spawn points, and food is hauled to the nearest one.

Rival Ant Controls

Food

  • Sugar: least filling, three drops to haul, one fed each
  • Fruit: five drops to haul, more filling, cures slowness
  • Protein: one heavy drop feeds two, adds speed and mating drive
  • Dead Insect: feeds five, takes three ants to haul
  • Spoiled: slows ants down, turns to poison
  • Poison: one drop poisons a crowd, incurable

Click the canvas to drop food, or pick a tool and drag to draw. Ants carry food they find back to the nest ring around their nearest spawn point. Once dropped off, only another ant can eat it.
