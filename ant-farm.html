Ant Controls

New ants appear at one of their colony's spawn points, and food is hauled to the nearest one.

Rival Ant Controls

Food

  • Sugar: least filling, three drops to haul, one fed each
  • Fruit: five drops to haul, more filling, cures slowness
  • Protein: one heavy drop feeds two, adds speed and mating drive
  • Dead Insect: feeds five, takes three ants to haul
  • Spoiled: slows ants down, turns to poison
  • Poison: one drop poisons a crowd, incurable

Click the canvas to drop food, or pick a tool and drag to draw. Ants carry food they find back to the nest ring around their nearest spawn point. Once dropped off, only another ant can eat it. Auto food drops and their speed live under Tuning.

Environment Tools

World Building

On: the colony auto-builds a sealed nest (entry, pantry, nursery, throne) linked by corridors through empty connector rooms; ants come and go only through the entry. Past ~10 ants a built nursery is needed to keep growing. Off: the classic sandbox.

Colony

Tuning

Live balance knobs — changes apply instantly and are saved. A * marks values that only affect newly born ants.

Configurations

Save your setup to load, export and compare later. A configuration stores the settings and spawn points; a full world also stores the current ants, food and terrain.
